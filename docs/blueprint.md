# **App Name**: Grubbie

## Core Features:

- User Authentication: Separate login/registration pages and dashboards for customers and vendors to manage their respective activities.
- Menu Browsing & Search: Customers can browse food items categorized into 'Cafeteria' (in-campus) and 'SouthPoint' (outside foods), with filtering and search capabilities.
- Order Placement: Customers can select items, specify quantities, and add them to a cart for pre-ordering.
- In-advance Order Scheduling: Customers can schedule orders for a future date and time.
- Cashless Payment Integration: Integration with Gcash and Paymaya for secure and convenient cashless transactions.
- Cash-on-pickup Payment: Customers can opt to pay with cash upon pickup of their order.
- Vendor Order Management: Vendors can view incoming orders, update order statuses (e.g., 'preparing', 'ready for pickup'), and manage their listed menu items.
- Customer Order Tracking: Customers can view the real-time status of their placed orders from preparation to pickup.
- Customer Reporting System: Vendors can report unruly customer behavior, triggering a review process.
- Admin Account Blocking: Administrators have the ability to block customer or vendor accounts based on reports or policy violations.
- Voluntary Account Deletion: Users can voluntarily delete their account from the settings, requiring them to type 'delete' to confirm the action.
- Food Item Ratings & Reviews: Customers can leave ratings and written reviews for individual food items and vendors, viewable by other customers.

## Style Guidelines:

- The primary color is a warm, inviting peach-orange (#DBAF70), reflecting a vibrant and appealing food experience. This color choice aims to be approachable and energetic, aligning with a student audience and a quick-service model.
- The background color is a very light, desaturated version of the primary hue (#F5F3F0), providing a clean, airy base that ensures readability and a sense of freshness, suitable for a light theme.
- The accent color is a rich, deep brown (#604020). This choice offers a strong, grounded contrast to the lighter primary colors and the light background, suggesting reliability and quality, while being aesthetically aligned with a food theme. It creates visual interest for key actions and branding elements.
- Headlines and prominent text elements will use 'Poppins', a geometric sans-serif, offering a contemporary, precise, and stylish feel suitable for capturing a student audience's attention.
- Body text and detailed information, such as menu descriptions, will use 'Inter', a grotesque-style sans-serif known for its legibility and modern neutrality, ensuring ease of reading on all screen sizes.
- Use clear, intuitive line icons for navigation and actions, specifically designed to convey food items, ordering processes, and payment methods at a glance.
- A responsive, card-based layout for menus will prioritize clear item display, large imagery for food visuals, and easy-to-use action buttons to facilitate quick pre-ordering on mobile devices.
- Subtle transition animations for adding items to the cart and order status updates will provide fluid user feedback and enhance the perceived speed and responsiveness of the app.
